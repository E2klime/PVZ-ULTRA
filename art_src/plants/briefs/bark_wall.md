# bark_wall

- **Role**: Cheap wall (4000 hp), cracks in 3 damage stages.
- **Botanical reference**: An old elm burl stump with bracket (shelf) fungi.
- **Twist**: The stump stands on its roots like a sumo wrestler; shelf fungi step up one side like armour plates, and each damage stage knocks off fungus and splits the bark.
- **Palette (≤5 + 1 accent)**: bark brown #7b5634, deep bark #4b3322, cut-wood tan #c49a62, moss #6f8f3a, fungus cream #e2cc96; accent: fungus orange edge #d4762c
- **Silhouette**: A wide, lopsided trapezoid: broad rooted base, a slanted cut top with growth rings, three shelf fungi stepping up the right side.
- **Personality**: Stoic, stubborn bouncer; heavy-lidded knot-hole eyes under a mossy brow; a tight-lipped bark split for a mouth.
- **Idle**: Barely-there breathing (body scale ±1.2%), moss brow twitch.
- **Attack tell**: none (passive wall).
- **Hit reaction**: Wobble; stage 1 = bark split and one fungus gone, stage 2 = deep crack, chipped top, a rueful look.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *elder_oak*: short and squat with no crown or canopy, no thorns, a cut top. vs *lily_raft*: upright and tall, brown, a solid block.
