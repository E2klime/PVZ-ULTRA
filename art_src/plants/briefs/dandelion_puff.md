# dandelion_puff

- **Role**: Cheap short-range AoE (14 dmg in 1.4 cells, every 2.5 s), hits neighbouring lanes.
- **Botanical reference**: Goatsbeard / salsify seed clock: a big, geometric see-through sphere of parachute seeds, bigger than a dandelion's.
- **Twist**: The seed clock sits in a spiky leaf ruff like a collar and fires volleys of seed darts that regrow instantly.
- **Palette (≤5 + 1 accent)**: seed white #f4f1e6, silver shadow #b9bccb, calyx green #6c9a45, ruff dark #3f6a3a, seed tips straw #d9c27a; accent: seed gold #f0c64a
- **Silhouette**: A large star-burst sphere on a tall thin stem, with a jagged leaf ruff under the sphere. The edge is spiky, not smooth.
- **Personality**: Twitchy, ticklish, a bit anxious; small eyes in the green calyx under the puff, tufted brows.
- **Idle**: The seed sphere trembles in the breeze; individual seeds shimmer.
- **Attack tell**: The sphere contracts (head scale -22%) then bursts back out.
- **Hit reaction**: Seeds flare outward and the eyes squeeze shut.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *sunbud*: see-through and spiky, white not gold, no leaf parasol. vs *lantern_bloom*: upright, not hanging, a sphere of spikes not a paper teardrop.
