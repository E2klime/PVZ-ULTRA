- **Twist**: A tiny ancient guardian tree: blackthorn spurs grow from its bark, the crown is a rounded cloud of oak leaves with a golden mistletoe sprig, and bark folds form a bearded elder's face.

- **Personality**: Wise, patient, slightly grumpy grandfather; deep-set eyes under a bark brow; moss beard.
- **Idle**: The crown sways slowly (canopy rotation); leaves rustle.
- **Attack tell**: none (passive); thorns are readable on the bark.
- **Hit reaction**: The crown shudders (wob) and a few leaves drop.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *bark_wall*: a living tree with a crown and thorns, taller, gold accent. vs *lantern_bloom*: a broad, symmetric crown, not a hanging lamp.
