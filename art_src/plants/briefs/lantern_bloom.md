# lantern_bloom

- **Role**: Support aura: +25% speed for the 8 surrounding plants (light tag).
- **Botanical reference**: Chinese lantern (Physalis alkekengi): a papery, ribbed calyx husk around a glowing berry.
- **Twist**: Hangs from a shepherd's-crook stem like a street lamp; the husk is half-skeletonised so the glowing berry (with the face) shows through the lace.
- **Palette (≤5 + 1 accent)**: husk orange #e2742c, paper light #f6b36a, vein brown #8a4422, stem #5f7a3a, leaf #44703a; accent: berry glow #ffd45a
- **Silhouette**: A tall arching crook stem with a teardrop lantern hanging from the tip. The only plant whose mass is at the top-right and hangs DOWN.
- **Personality**: Kindly night-watchman; soft eyes on the berry visible through the lace window; content smile.
- **Idle**: The lantern swings like a pendulum and the glow pulses.
- **Attack tell**: none (passive aura); the glow pulse is the readable signal.
- **Hit reaction**: A sharp swing and a flicker of the glow.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *sunbud*: hangs from a crook instead of sitting on a stem; orange paper, not gold petals. vs *dandelion_puff*: a solid, closed teardrop, not a spiky sphere.
