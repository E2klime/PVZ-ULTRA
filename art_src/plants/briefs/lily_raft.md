# lily_raft

- **Role**: Water platform: other plants stand on it; it survives if they are eaten.
- **Botanical reference**: Giant Amazon water lily (Victoria) pad with an upturned rim.
- **Twist**: The rim is folded up like a pie crust and a single pink bud rides at the back like a figurehead.
- **Palette (≤5 + 1 accent)**: pad green #5f9a3e, rim red #a2463e, vein light #9cc964, underside dark #3e6234, bud pink #f1a2b8; accent: bud highlight #ffd2e0
- **Silhouette**: A flat wide ellipse seen at 3/4 from above, with a raised scalloped rim and one bud at the back-left. Low; tops out below 40 px on screen.
- **Personality**: No face: it's furniture. Personality from the bud bobbing like a curious head.
- **Idle**: Bobs on the water (body offset).
- **Attack tell**: none.
- **Hit reaction**: Dips and splashes.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *thorn_carpet*-type flats: round with a raised red rim and water sheen, no spikes. vs *bark_wall*: flat and green, not upright.
