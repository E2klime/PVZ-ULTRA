# pod_shooter

- **Role**: Basic single-lane shooter (20 dmg / 1.45 s).
- **Botanical reference**: Broad-bean / runner-bean pod: a long, slightly curved fleshy pod with seed bulges.
- **Twist**: The pod is mounted sideways like a little field cannon; its split tip is the muzzle and the next beans are visible queued inside the seam.
- **Palette (≤5 + 1 accent)**: pod green #6aa83a, deep pod #3e7230, lime light #b6d65a, bean cream #e8dca0, stem #4f7a35; accent: muzzle-lip red-brown #a2432a
- **Silhouette**: A horizontal banana-shaped pod pointing right on a short, thick stem with two curling tendrils on top. Low and long, unlike every round-headed plant.
- **Personality**: Gruff little artillerist; a single leaf over the eyes acts like a cap brim; squinting aim.
- **Idle**: The pod bobs gently; tendrils curl and uncurl.
- **Attack tell**: The pod recoils backwards (pose_kick) and the seam bulges just before the shot.
- **Hit reaction**: The tendrils flare, the pod jerks up, a short squint.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *snapper_trap*: a closed horizontal tube, no open mouth, green not magenta. vs *sunbud*: long and horizontal, not a round cup.
