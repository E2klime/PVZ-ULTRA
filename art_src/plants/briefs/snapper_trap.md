# snapper_trap

- **Role**: Single-target trap: swallows the zombie in front, then chews for 40 s.
- **Botanical reference**: Snapdragon (Antirrhinum) flower: a two-lipped tube flower that 'snaps' when squeezed.
- **Twist**: A giant snapdragon head whose lower lip is a hinged jaw; the spotted throat is the gullet; a spire of smaller buds stands behind it like a crest.
- **Palette (≤5 + 1 accent)**: petal magenta #b04c9a, deep plum #5e2a5e, throat pink #f29ab5, spot yellow #f2cf4a, stem/leaves #4f7d3a; accent: throat yellow spots #ffe066
- **Silhouette**: A forward-leaning dragon-like flower head with an open hinged lower lip, on a stem with a tall spike of buds rising behind it. Asymmetric, pointing right.
- **Personality**: Greedy, theatrical, smug; heavy brows over narrow eyes on the upper lip; licks its lip.
- **Idle**: The jaw slack-breathes; the bud spire sways.
- **Attack tell**: The jaw drops wide open (jaw rotation) before the snap.
- **Hit reaction**: The head flinches back, a quick indignant blink.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *pod_shooter*: an open two-lipped mouth, magenta, with a vertical bud spire. vs *dandelion_puff*: solid petal head, not see-through.
