# sunbud

- **Role**: Sun producer (25 sun / 24 s). Cheapest economy plant, planted in rows of 5–10 early.
- **Botanical reference**: Globe-flower (Trollius) bud: a closed, cupped sphere of overlapping waxy petals.
- **Twist**: The cup is a little sun-oven: the petals part at the top and a warm ember of light swells inside before a sun pops out.
- **Palette (≤5 + 1 accent)**: petal gold #f2b632, deep amber #c9771e, sap green #5f9a3a, shadow leaf #3d6b34, stem olive #7c8f3c; accent: inner glow #fff1a8
- **Silhouette**: A ball-topped S-curve: a cupped sphere leaning forward on a stem that curls once, with ONE broad parasol leaf held out to the left like an umbrella. No radiating petals.
- **Personality**: Sleepy, content sunbather; eyes half-lidded, peeking out between the two front petals.
- **Idle**: Slow breathing swell of the cup; the parasol leaf sways out of phase.
- **Attack tell**: The cup glows from inside and the petals part (produce clip, head scale +8%).
- **Hit reaction**: The cup snaps shut and squashes (wob), the leaf flinches.
- **Differs from its 2 nearest neighbours**: vs *dandelion_puff*: a solid closed cup, not a spiky see-through sphere; gold, not white. vs *lantern_bloom*: stands upright on its stem instead of hanging, round instead of a teardrop.
